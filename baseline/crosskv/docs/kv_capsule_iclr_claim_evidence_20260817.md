## Current defensible paper thesis

> Normal answer generation writes a portable lexical continuation state into
> Tail-KV.  Before any answer token exists, a small sender-only writer can instead
> emit four native, RoPE-relocatable KV positions and serialize selected layers
> as an actual INT4 packet.  These positions are a depth-indexed state channel,
> not four interchangeable vectors: early layers inject compact functional state
> that downstream queries transform, while deeper layers increasingly restore
> lexical/readout state.  Across Qwen3-8B and Llama-3.1-8B, and from MuSiQue to
> HotpotQA and untouched 2WikiMQA, 68--76 KB depth-selected packets carry causal
> source state.  On dependent reordered documents, token-level local repair
> requires nearly complete recomputation, whereas the learned packet remains a
> distinct low-payload point on a task-dependent quality frontier.

This is now supported by direct KVPacket and CacheBlend baselines, a
full-recompute CacheBlend recovery gate, a real nibble codec, a fresh MuSiQue
confirm set, exact end-to-end first16 replication on 2WikiMQA, a separately
sealed 300-case 2Wiki-E causal confirmation, and three frozen multi-budget
negative controls.  The stop-gradient confirmation changes the
mechanism conclusion: early and deep state are functionally different, but
useful early state still depends on joint full-depth credit assignment.  An
emission-aligned sender bottleneck concentrates in-domain state but forgets
cross-task semantics when all capsule parameters are updated.  The subsequent
frozen-base intervention keeps K/V layers 0--14 bitwise identical and writes a
boundary residual only into layer 15.  The representation has since been split
on the wire: a 67,642-B immutable base plus an independently coded 4,252-B task
delta preserves or slightly improves the direct packet on both MuSiQue and
2Wiki, while exposing a true 6.29% incremental base-hit payload.  Production
FP16-scale codec measurements supersede the earlier fake-INT4 claim of a
significant rank-16 gain over base.

Canonical-RoPE packing further proves that target-independent packet bytes are
possible: the same document hashes identically under different receiver
prefixes.  Its strict 2Wiki safety CI misses by about .0033, however, and the
predeclared equal-byte polar repair fails on untouched confirmation rows.  A
K8/V4 precision control passes a new MuSiQue confirmation but fails to repair
2Wiki despite an 85% tensor-error reduction and 48% more bytes, showing that
tensor fidelity is not a reliable proxy for decoder equivalence.
The subsequent fixed-rate positional-orbit objective converges and preserves
target/canonical behavior on MultifieldQA, but fails its frozen source-causal
and repair-DiD gates.  This shifts the limiting factor from positional codec
fidelity to cross-task semantic state encoding and receiver use.
Consequently the paper can claim short causal state and composable
base-plus-delta reuse, but not yet universal receiver-independent behavioral
safety.  It is not yet a complete ICLR package: the capsule still loses
substantial quality to KVPacket/plaintext on several workloads, RelayCaching
and InfoFlow KV remain untested, cross-model transfer is absent, and real
link/concurrency/serving evidence is missing.
